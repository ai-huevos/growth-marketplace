## 3. THE OFFER ARCHITECTURE (ESCALA Value Ladder)

The offering follows a 4-tier ascension ladder. Each tier feeds the next. The economics compound upward.

### Tier 1: BAIT — "AI Energy Diagnostic" (FREE)

- **Format**: Interactive online quiz (10 questions, 5-7 minutes)
- **Deliverable**: Personalized Energy Score (0-100) across 4 dimensions + 3 specific quick wins
- **4 Score Dimensions**: Data Flow (0-25), Real Adoption (0-25), Visibility (0-25), Team Capacity (0-25)
- **Headline**: "Your company has hidden AI energy. Discover it in 5 minutes."
- **Ascension trigger**: Score < 50 creates urgency → "You have trapped energy in X, Y, Z — a Discovery Sprint unlocks it in 2 weeks."
- **Target metrics**: 100-200 leads/month at CPL < $15 | 20-35% conversion to Frontend

### Tier 2: FRONTEND — "Discovery Sprint" ($500–$1,500)

- **Format**: 2-week organizational archaeology with 1 embedded KAI Partner (8-12 total hours)
- **Deliverable**: **Energy Map** — visual document showing where information flows (or doesn't), 3-7 quantified friction points, estimated annual savings per friction point, and a prioritized automation roadmap
- **Process**: Days 1-3: Stakeholder interviews | Days 4-7: Real data flow mapping | Days 8-10: Energy Map delivery + findings presentation
- **Ascension trigger**: The Energy Map quantifies the cost of NOT acting → "If you lose $X every quarter from these bottlenecks... when do we start?"
- **Target metrics**: 25-40% conversion from lead to Sprint client

### Tier 3: MIDDLE — "AI-OS Build" ($5,000–$15,000)

- **Format**: 90-day implementation program with 2-3 embedded KAI Partners
- **3 Phases**: Month 1: Rails (connection infrastructure) → Month 2: Flow (data moving, team operating) → Month 3: Energy (optimization, measured ROI)
- **Deliverable**: Functional AI-OS on top of existing infrastructure, with automations in production, trained team, and documented ROI
- **Reference case**: "Finkargo: from 45 minutes per transaction to 3 minutes. $15M in savings. 110+ automated processes in 90 days."
- **Ascension trigger**: Proven ROI in one department → "What if we do this in logistics, finance, HR?"
- **Target metrics**: 30-50% ascension from Frontend | 55-65% gross margin

### Tier 4: BACKEND — "Continuous Partner" ($10,000–$25,000/month)

- **Format**: Monthly retainer, 12-month minimum, 1-2 dedicated KAI Partners
- **Deliverable**: Expansion to new departments, continuous optimization, monthly ROI reports, quarterly reviews
- **Proof point**: Finkargo — 3+ years of continuous relationship. From 1 process to 110+. From $35K investment to $650M in processed transactions.
- **Target metrics**: 40-60% ascension from Middle | <10% annual churn | $180K-$600K LTV per client

## 4. THE GROWTH OPERATING SYSTEM (4-Phase Model)

GrowthOS diagnoses the user's bottleneck and routes them to the right phase:

```
DEFINE → ATTRACT → CONVERT → SCALE
```

**Skills activated**: Customer Success Ops, Renewal & Expansion, Coaching (ROCA), Sales Transformation (90-day), Funnel Optimization
**Output**: NRR >100%, reduced churn, measured team capacity

## 7. BRAND VOICE RULES

### Voice Priority Order

```
BOLD → SMART → WARM → RELIABLE
```

Bold leads: impact first, data second, narrative third.

### Do / Don't

| Rule | ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|------|---------|
| Lead with impact | "$650M in transactions processed by 4 people" | "After exhaustive analysis, opportunities were identified..." |
| Short sentences | "Systems were disconnected. Information didn't flow. Nobody knew what was happening." | "The lack of integration between systems generated a deficit in operational visibility..." |
| Name the problem | "Your team operates on WhatsApp and Excel. That doesn't scale." | "There are areas for improvement in communication processes..." |
| Numbers up front | "44 interviews. 5 departments. 3 bottlenecks." | "A comprehensive interview process was conducted across multiple areas..." |
| Active voice | "We build your rails." | "The rails were built by our team." |
| Close with action | "Book a session. Let's find your hidden energy." | "If you'd like more information, please don't hesitate to reach out." |

### Brand Vocabulary

| ✅ Use | ❌ Never Use |
|--------|------------|
| Connect | "End-to-end solution" |
| Build rails | "Leverage" / "Synergy" |
| Energy, flow, capture | "Disruptive" |
| Partner | "Robust" |
| AI-OS / Operating System | "World-class" |
| Archaeology | "Cutting-edge" / "State-of-the-art" |
| "We stay." | "Seamless" |

### Narrative Arc: GOLPE

Every piece of content follows this structure:

```
1. GOLPE  → Shocking data point or raw problem
2. DOLOR  → Why it matters (real business consequence)
3. PUENTE → How we solve it
4. PRUEBA → Real case / metric / testimonial
5. ACCIÓN → What to do now (concrete CTA)
```

**Example**:
```
GOLPE:  "Your company spends $2M/year on AI nobody uses."
DOLOR:  "Every quarter your team reports 'progress' on adoption. Reality: they're still in Excel."
PUENTE: "We build your AI-OS with your team, using your real data — not generic demos."
PRUEBA: "Finkargo: from 45 min per transaction to 3 min. $650M processed."
ACCIÓN: "30 minutes. We'll show you where the hidden energy in your operation lives."
```
