## TYPE: MANIFESTOR

**Population:** ~8% of humanity
**Animal:** Elephant — the real kings of the jungle who lead by peacefully doing their own thing

### Core design
- You initiate. You are the cause; everyone else is the effect.
- Fire starter — you get things started for others to join in
- Flick the first domino; create waves and movements, whether intentionally or not
- Energy is felt when you walk into a room
- Don't need to wait for collaboration from the Universe to act
- Impulsive energy driven by the Divine expressing itself through you
- Your signs are internal, not external
- Structures and "shoulds" are awful for this type — must check if internal "shoulds" are actually external conditioning

### Energy pattern
- Spikes of energy, quickest of any type — once there's an urge, act, then rest
- **Not good for 9-5 jobs**
- Great for intense projects
- Act on impulse then **rest is key**
- The "do do do" counteracts ability to receive from the divine
- Best to act on 1–2 urges at a time
- It's about quality of what you do, not how much — less doing takes you much further

### Urges vs preferences
- **Urge:** A feeling that comes out of nowhere, cannot be explained, no logic process before it. Your sign to ACT. Action becomes effortless.
- **Preference:** Everything else. Can be compromised on without making yourself small.

### Common challenge
- Selective aura — attracts some people, repels others
- This can feel like rejection/disapproval, leading many Manifestors to spend life trying to please everyone and stay small
- Not the correct use of the energy
- Can create change from an intention where "for you to win, nobody else has to lose"
- **Remove your worth from how much you do for others**
- Rise yourself; others rise as a side effect

## STRATEGY: INFORMING

**"Here's where I'm going."**

- Inform once you have made the decision but before you act on it
- No micro-management, no dragging, no handholding
- If unsure who needs to know, it's usually someone directly related to or impacted by the decision
- Your energy is naturally selective and protected — you make yourself available for the right people when you make the first move
- You are here to have an impact — by seeing initiating as the gift it is

**Sweet spot sequence:**
1. Trust what your decision is (using your authority)
2. Consider who will be impacted
3. Inform all those beings (not asking permission — letting them know)
4. Then act

## AUTHORITY: EMOTIONAL (SOLAR PLEXUS)

**Decision rule:** Wait until cool, calm, and collected to make a decision. There is NO truth/clarity in the now. Buy yourself time.

### The emotional wave
- May wake up in a good mood for no reason, or a bad mood for no reason
- Emotional processing lives in the body, not the brain
- Low moods bring gifts — creativity, inspiration, compassion, empathy, emotional intelligence
- **Escalator pattern:** Up and up and up (life is beautiful, incrementally) then a fall out of nowhere

### Emotional Manifestor specifics
- Most volatile Manifestor variant
- Don't have to wait but should honor the emotional wave
- When making a decision, will feel like you're missing information — this will prevent moving forward until the wave completes
- Can seek additional information (talk to someone) to process the wave
