# AI HUEVOS — FOUNDER MEDIA ENGINE
*v1.0 · April 2026*

The media engine is **not a side project**. It is the marketing function for AI Huevos — the channel that generates Free Diagnostics, the demonstration of capability, and the showcase Daniel needs ("show what I've done"). Every interview is a sales asset, a content asset, and a relationship asset.

---

## THESIS

> *"B2B founders in LATAM don't need another podcast about generic startup advice. They need to see, in 45 minutes, exactly how a peer founder solved the problem they're stuck on right now — and they need to leave with a concrete next step."*

That's the show.

---

## FORMAT

### Episode structure (45 minutes recorded, ~30 minutes published)
| Block | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 | Cold intro: who's the guest, what's the specific problem they solved |
| 2 | 5–20 | The story: how they hit the wall, what they tried, what worked |
| 3 | 20–35 | The system: what's running today, with numbers, no marketing fluff |
| 4 | 35–40 | Live diagnostic moment: Daniel asks ONE pointed question, guest answers honestly |
| 5 | 40–45 | The takeaway: 3 actions the audience can do this Monday |

**Why this format:**
- Mirrors the masterclass session blocks (PULSO framework already validated)
- Shows AI Huevos's diagnostic motion live (the live diagnostic in block 4 = sales asset)
- Generates 3-action takeaway = lead magnet hook for Free Diagnostic CTA

### Production stack
- **Recording:** Riverside.fm or StreamYard (separate audio tracks, video, remote-friendly)
- **Editing:** Opus Pro (AI clip extraction) + light human polish
- **Distribution:** YouTube long-form, LinkedIn carousel, Spotify audio, IG/TikTok clips, newsletter
- **Total time per episode:** Daniel 90 min (recording + intro/outro), editor 4–6 hrs

## VOICE / EDITORIAL RULES

- **Spanish primary, English bilingual where appropriate.** Latino owner-operators search in Spanish; the moat is Spanish-first authority.
- **Daniel-shaped voice:** paisa-authentic, "métale candela," "comer mierda," contrarian data hooks. Don't sand the edges off. The Endeavor postmortem confirmed this is the brand.
- **No marketing fluff.** Specific numbers, specific tactics, specific timelines. The audience analysis showed founders explicitly hate "modelo bonito" with no actionable detail.
- **Vulnerability calibrated.** Daniel's "I'm not an expert in anything, my math is bad" works. False humility doesn't.
- **CTA every time.** No exceptions. The masterclass postmortem's biggest lesson: enrollment without close = generosity without revenue.
