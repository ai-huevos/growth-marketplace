## THE 5 STRATEGIC QUESTIONS (canonical)

These are Daniel's already-validated questions from the masterclass diagnostic-en-vivo with Cristian/Didactical, the audit calls with Cialne / Wiltech / G3, and the cluster analysis. They cover the dimensions that determine maturity and offer routing.

### 1. ECONOMICS
> *"What's your current revenue and ARR? How fast did you grow last year?"*
**What it tells you:** Company stage, budget capacity, urgency.

### 2. SALES CYCLE
> *"How long is your average sales cycle? How many stakeholders typically participate in a decision?"*
**What it tells you:** Complexity of motion, attribution challenge, why leads stall.

### 3. WHO SELLS
> *"Who's responsible for sales today? Founder? A hire? Ad-hoc?"*
**What it tells you:** Maturity level. Founder = Nivel 0–1. Dedicated person without metrics = Nivel 1. Team with CRM and pipeline = Nivel 2+.

### 4. LEAD SOURCES (specific)
> *"Where do leads come from THIS WEEK? Be specific."*
**What it tells you:** Whether they have channels or just contacts. Whether attribution exists.

### 5. SCALE BREAK
> *"What breaks first when you double in size?"*
**What it tells you:** What they actually fear / what's holding them back. Pain point for the close.

## THE 30-MIN STRUCTURE

| Min | What | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Warm-up + permission | "Voy a hacerte 5 preguntas. Después te digo qué encontré, qué te recomiendo, y qué cuesta. Sin venta. ¿Vale?" |
| 3–18 | The 5 questions (3 minutes each) | Listen for maturity signals. Take notes in your head, not on paper. |
| 18–25 | The mirror | "Esto es lo que te oí. Estás en Nivel X. Tienes los siguientes 3 problemas que importan. Aquí está el orden de magnitud." |
| 25–30 | The recommendation | "Lo que te conviene es **[Offer N]**. Cuesta **[$range]**. Toma **[duration]**. Aquí está cómo se vería el primer mes. ¿Te tiene sentido?" |

**The close is built in.** No separate "would you like to talk again?" — the recommendation IS the offer. They either say yes / let me think / not now. Each branch has a follow-up.

## QUALIFYING DISQUALIFIERS (kill the call early)

Save your time. End the call within 10 minutes if any of these:

- ❌ Pre-revenue (under $500K ARR) — they need a product, not a sales system
- ❌ B2C consumer — wrong ICP, refer out
- ❌ E-commerce / physical product — out of scope, refer out
- ❌ Capital question (raising a round, not building an engine) — refer to Cristian's framework
- ❌ Founder doesn't show up to the call — DO NOT reschedule more than once
- ❌ "We'll think about it and let you know" without specifics — set a time-bound follow-up or close the loop

**The discipline:** if it's not a fit, name it kindly and end the call. *"Lo que necesitas no es lo que hacemos. Lo que hacemos es X. Para tu caso, te recomiendo [refer]. Si en 6 meses cambia tu situación, me llamas."* That's a referral asset, not a lost lead.
