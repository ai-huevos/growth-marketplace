### Kolbe zones defined

- **Initiate (8–10):** Proactive, drives the mode, this is where you push
- **ReAct (4–7):** Responsive, accommodates both directions, adapts to context
- **CounterAct (1–3):** Counteracts / resists / prevents — has energy to push *against*, not maintain. Not a weakness — a different form of energy direction.

## CONABLES TIPS (Kolbe's direct recommendations)

**Time and energy:**
- Commit — but to very little. Target your top priorities.
- Seize the moment when your ideas surface. Don't wait or debate.
- After working intensely to meet deadlines, take time to Do Nothing.
- **Self-Provoke to get where you want to go. You're responsible for goading yourself to initiate necessary action.**

**Productivity:**
- You're likely to procrastinate if you have to fill in lots of forms.
- When you need to be persuasive in an unfamiliar situation, wing it.
- You need to vary activities to keep your mind engaged.
- A good way to start your day is to check and adjust priorities.

**Communication:**
- People get it wrong when they think you'll take on any dare. You strive when you're working on deadline.
- Say it rather than write it. Over-prepping takes you out of your stride. Brief notes give you all you need.
- Use humor — it's your best bet for bridging conative gaps.

**Look for roles that let you:**
- Review, confirm, report, substantiate, amend (FF 5)
- Diversify, disguise, diffuse, randomize, switch (FT 2)
- Speculate, discover, originate, promote, experiment (QS 9)
- Conceptualize, symbolize, imagine, virtualize, visualize (IMP 3)

**Kolbe warning:**
> "Steer away from careers where your CounterActing abilities will be perceived negatively."

Your Follow Thru and Implementor are both CounterAct — meaning roles that demand sustained maintenance, procedural adherence, or hands-on physical construction will feel adversarial to your instinct.
