## TWO ENTRY OPTIONS (new — addresses the "cheaper start" ask)

### Option A — Full 6-Month Engagement (recommended)

Everything in V1.0 stays:
- **$16,000 fee fixed** (Mes 1 setup + 5 mensualidades)
- **10% commission** on net new revenue attributed to AI Huevos channels
- **Bundle estratégico included** (the full strategic work valued at $6,500 standalone)
- **Sector exclusivity** — único cliente Energy Tech LATAM during engagement
- **Cláusula de salida** — sin penalidad al cierre del Mes 4 si no hay leads calificados

This is the **Growth Stack LATAM standard tier** for a 6-month commitment. Best economics for Energy Master, deepest engagement, best perf-fee math.

### Option B — 60-Day Validation Sprint (cheaper start)

For Energy Master to validate the system before committing to 6 months:

- **$5,000 fee** for 60 days
- **Includes:**
  - **Diagnóstico GTM** (the same one in the Bundle, full report)
  - **HubSpot vs GoHighLevel decision** with cost-benefit analysis (same Mes 1 deliverable)
  - **CRM optimization** of current HubSpot to 80%+ utilization (was at 20%)
  - **First Soap Opera Sequence** running with Alejandro as Attractive Character
  - **Plan de Inversión Detallado** (same Mes 1 critical deliverable, deliverable to board)
  - **Two leadership sessions** with Daniel
- **Conversion math:** if Energy Master converts to Option A at end of 60 days, the **$5,000 paid for the sprint counts as Mes 1 of the 6-month engagement** — total still $16,000, no double-pay.
- If Energy Master does NOT convert, the deliverables (Plan de Inversión, GTM diagnostic, optimized HubSpot) remain theirs. No further obligation.

This gives Alejandro the lower-commitment entry he asked for, without us discounting the full engagement and anchoring AI Huevos low for the next deal.
