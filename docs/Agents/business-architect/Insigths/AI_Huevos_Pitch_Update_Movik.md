## WHAT CHANGED

V2 / V3 framed the engagement as "Marketing Deployment Machine" — a custom 9-week scope for $2,500/mo retainer + 8% revenue performance fee.

**Two issues with that framing:**
1. The 8% revenue share is blocked by Movik's regulatory structure as a lending company.
2. The custom scope made the deal look like consulting bespoke work — Movik couldn't price it against anything.

**V4 keeps everything Tomás and the Movik team already aligned on (the work), repositions it as a productized offer (the structure), and replaces the perf-fee with a per-carrier-onboarded bonus that works under their lending-company constraints.**

## THE OFFER (PRODUCTIZED)

### Movik fits AI Huevos's Scale Ops Audit shape — at the upper end ($12–15K) for the build, with operating retainer to follow.

This is offer #4 in our standard pricing grid (`AI_Huevos_Pricing_Grid.md`). It's how we sell this engagement to other companies too. Movik gets the same product everyone else gets, calibrated to their scale.

### Phase 1 — Scale Ops Audit + Foundation Build (6 weeks)
- **Capacity audit** — process map, bottleneck inventory, FMCSA pipeline analysis, partner program mapping, lead-magnet attribution architecture
- **AI agent build** — 3 prioritized agents from the audit (FMCSA pipeline qualifier, follow-up sequencer, partner-referral tracker)
- **CRM live** — GoHighLevel implementation with three-channel tagging (Field Sales / Strategic Alliances / Digital), source attribution
- **Performance dashboard** — channel-level KPIs, pipeline value, attribution cleared

### Price: $12,000–$15,000
- 50% on signing
- 30% on Week 4 milestone (CRM + audit complete)
- 20% on Week 6 handoff (agents live + dashboard)

### Phase 2 — Operate (months 2 onward)
Conversion to **Growth Stack LATAM** retainer (offer #3):
- **$2,500–$3,500 / month** retainer (operate + optimize)
- Includes: Daniel as fractional CMO + Magic Hack ongoing CRM ops + Santiago AI agents maintenance + content engine + monthly board-grade report

### Performance fee — restructured for lending compliance
**Replace 8% revenue share with: $X per carrier onboarded through Channels 2 and 3 (Strategic Alliances + Digital).**

Rationale Movik can take to legal:
- Not tied to the financial product or revenue → not a kickback
- Per-customer-acquisition fee, indistinguishable from any other CAC-based marketing spend
- Capped at growth-target hit per quarter

**Suggested structure:**
- **$200–$400 per carrier onboarded via Ch2 (Strategic Alliances)** — these are the higher-value 3PL referrals
- **$50–$150 per carrier onboarded via Ch3 (Digital / Inbound)** — these are the higher-volume, lower-CAC channel
- **Cap:** total perf fee not to exceed 50% of monthly retainer in any month
- **Floor:** retainer-only ("retainer solo, cero fee") if growth target missed in a month

For Movik's 2026 target (12 carriers via Ch3, 23 via Ch2 = 35 net new from AI Huevos channels): max perf fee at $400 × 23 + $150 × 12 = **$11,000/year on top of $30–42K retainer**. Total Movik investment ~$50–60K for the year. Modeled against the $250M pipeline this engine builds for 2027 (per the Growth Strategy PDF), this is rounding error.
