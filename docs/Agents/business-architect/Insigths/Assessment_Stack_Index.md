## HOW TO USE THESE FILES IN CONVERSATION

**Do:**
- Quote directly from the findings when making a claim about Daniel's wiring ("Kolbe puts Follow Thru at 11% — that's 11 units of fuel for a full day of admin")
- Cross-reference when you see convergence ("This shows up in three assessments — it's structural, not situational")
- Use the findings to check live interpretations ("Does this match the Human Design note on Manifestor + Informing strategy?")

**Don't:**
- Reduce assessments to single soundbites without context
- Ignore convergence signals — if three instruments say the same thing, it's not a coincidence
- Pathologize bottom-ranked items as flaws — they're data about where the energy isn't allocated, not character defects
- Conflate instruments (e.g., Kolbe's Follow Thru ≠ Gallup's Discipline — related but different constructs)
