**Nivel 0–1 founders.** They sell by relationship. The pipeline depends on first-degree contacts. When those run out, sales drops to zero. They have a great product/service, no system around it.

**Empirical signal:** 75% of the masterclass audience is at this level. 52% of all responses flagged "no sales system" as the top pain.

### Promise
*"In 8 weeks, you will have a sales system that doesn't depend on you remembering who to follow up with. CRM live, pipeline measurable, automations running, your first 3 months supervised so it sticks."*

### Scope (fixed)
- **Discovery (Week 1)** — ICP definition, current funnel mapping, 3-cluster audit (Motor Comercial / Demanda / Operación), revenue economics

- **Content & Capture (Week 6)** — 3 lead magnets specified, landing pages live, attribution tagged, WhatsApp + email channels integrated
- **Handoff (Week 7)** — Training sessions for client team, dashboard walkthrough, playbook PDF
- **Accompaniment (Months 2–4)** — Monthly review, quarterly tune-up, async Slack channel

### Deliverables
1. **Diagnostic Report** — Where they are, where they're stuck, the 3 obstacles to their next 50 leads
2. **CRM live** with sales pipeline, lead scoring, and source attribution
3. **6 automation sequences** running (named above)
4. **3 lead magnets** + landing pages
5. **Dashboard** showing pipeline value, conversion rates, source attribution
6. **Playbook PDF** — the sales motion documented for their team
7. **3 months of monthly reviews** + async support

### Price & terms
- **$5,000–$8,000** project fee (sliding by complexity: number of products, channels, integrations needed)
- 50% on signing, 50% on Week 6 handoff
- 3 months accompaniment included (months 2–4 post-handoff)
- Optional: convert to **Growth Stack LATAM** retainer (Offer 3) at month 4

### Delivery team

- **Magic Hack (Sebastián)** — CRM/GHL build, automations, integrations

### Sales talk track

Eighty percent of B2B founders we talk to built a product but never built a system around it — they sell by relationship until the relationships run out. We architect the system, install it in 8 weeks, and stay with you for 3 months to make sure it sticks. $5–8K depending on complexity. We start with a free 30-minute diagnostic to scope you."*

## THE FREE DIAGNOSTIC (THE WEDGE)

The diagnostic is **always free** — it's the qualifier that routes prospects to the right offer.

**Two modes:**

### Mode A — Live 30-minute diagnostic call
The proven motion. Daniel does it 1:1 on Zoom or in-person. Audit close rate: 9 of 11 in last 90 days converted to next-step.

### Mode B — WhatsApp conversational AI diagnostic
The leverage tool Santiago is building. Same logic, scaled. Founder messages WhatsApp number, AI agent walks them through 5–7 strategic questions, Daniel reviews the output and books a follow-up where qualified.

**The 5 strategic questions** (from Daniel's already-validated motion):
1. What's your current revenue and ARR?
2. How long is your sales cycle? How many stakeholders?
3. Who's responsible for sales today? (Founder? Hire? Ad-hoc?)
4. Where do leads come from this week? (Be specific.)
5. What breaks first when you double in size?

**The output** (delivered same-day):
- Maturity level (0, 1, 2, or 3)
- Top 3 obstacles to their next 50 leads
- Recommended offer (1, 2, 3, or 4)
- Concrete pricing range
- Calendar link to book the proposal session
