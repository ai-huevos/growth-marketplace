## Divergence Analysis (Where They Differ)

### D1: Founder DNA — HOLD vs BUY
| Canonical | Independent |

**Analysis:** The independent agent is MORE cautious because it weights Danny's relational/emotional DNA heavily and notes that "KAI" carries Daniel's energy theme but gives Danny less to hold. The canonical version resolves this by noting "Partners = human relationship (Danny's world)." The independent version doesn't find that resolution as complete.

**Founder action needed:** Danny must feel KAI is HIS word too, not just Daniel's. The "nos quedamos" / "AI que se queda" tagline direction from the independent agent could bridge this.

### D2: Portuguese "Cair" Risk — NEW finding
The independent Linguistic Analyst surfaced a risk not in the canonical panel: in Brazilian Portuguese, "cai" (pronounced identically to KAI) means "falls/collapses." The canonical panel missed this entirely.

**Action needed:** Brief Brazilian market copy with awareness. Avoid "KAI cai" constructions. Not a blocker but requires copywriting discipline for Brazil expansion.

### D3: Spelling/Recall in Oral Spanish — NEW finding
The independent Linguistic Analyst flagged: when a Mexican CFO dictates "send the contract to KAI Partners," the assistant may type "Kay Partners" or "Cai Partners." The canonical panel didn't address this oral disambiguation risk.

**Action needed:** Establish pronunciation anchor (brief origin story) in first 18 months. Always present the full written name first before verbal references.

### D4: SEO Namespace — Emphasized differently
Both panels note KAI is used by other companies. The canonical Devil's Advocate says "mitigation: strong visual identity + Partners suffix." The independent Devil's Advocate is MORE concerned: "you cannot build a clean SEO moat" and rates trademark risk 4/10.

**Action needed:** Trademark search in CO, MX, US, EU this week. Domain acquisition before any public-facing use.

### D5: "Partners" Aging Risk — Scale question
The independent Brand Strategist raises: "at 40 people and 200 clients, 'Partners' can start to feel like false intimacy." The canonical panel doesn't address this long-term concern.

**Action needed:** Pressure-test the name against 3-year scale target. If the vision is 15-person firm with deep accounts, "Partners" holds. If 150-person company with product line, may need future evolution.
