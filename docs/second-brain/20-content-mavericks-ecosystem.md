## Part 1: Human-Readable Overview

### 1. Framework Ecosystem Map

The Content Mavericks ecosystem consists of seven interconnected frameworks:

#### **Framework 1: Andrew & Pete Content Mavericks (Original Model)**
The foundational 7-step process that established the content marketing philosophy.[^2][^5][^6]

**Philosophy**: Create content that makes people "happier or smarter" to build trust by showing (not telling) expertise.

**7 Steps**:
1. **Content Mindset** - Adopt the philosophy that content builds trust through demonstration
2. **Content Branding** - Develop recognizable branded elements in content
3. **Content Stamp** - Create instantly recognizable content signatures
4. **Content Creation** - Produce value-driven content systematically
5. **Content Organization** - Structure content assets for maximum leverage
6. **Content Promotion** - Distribute strategically beyond organic reach
7. **Content Lead Generation** - Convert content consumers into leads and customers

**Target User**: New marketers, entrepreneurs starting content marketing, or established marketers with stale content strategies.

**Typical Outcomes**: Brand recognition, shareable content, reduced reliance on paid reach, trust-based selling.

#### **Framework 2: Ski Slope Strategy**
A 3-step framework for turning blogs into 7-figure revenue channels within 12-24 months.[^7][^8][^9][^10][^1]

**Core Concept**: Content difficulty progression (Green Circle → Blue Square → Black Diamond) mirroring ski slope difficulty levels.

**3-Step System**:

**Step 1: Build Topic Triangle**
- **Green Circle Content**: Easy-entry blog posts (long-tail SEO, TOFU)
- **Blue Square Content**: Lead magnets, tools, webinars (MOFU)
- **Black Diamond Content**: Case studies, comparison pages, ROI calculators (BOFU)

**Step 2: Promote Linkable Assets**
- Build domain authority through strategic link building
- Focus on "Masterpiece Content" (3k+ words, must be #1 on internet for topic)

**Step 3: Create Content Marketing Funnel**
- TOFU → MOFU → BOFU progression
- Internal linking structure (silos/content clusters)

**Target User**: Entrepreneurs with proven offers wanting scalable traffic growth.

**Typical Outcomes**: Consistent revenue growth compounding like interest, hard-to-compete-with marketing moat, predictable lead flow.

#### **Framework 3: More Traffic Less Content (MTLC)**
A $5 training teaching how to attract $3k-$15k/month clients using single blog posts.[^3]

**Core Philosophy**: "Less Is More" - leverage fewer, higher-quality pieces vs content volume.

**7 Core Modules**:

1. **Greatest Hits Survey** (0:06:30)
   - One question to audience/group
   - Outputs 5 blog post topics in 30 minutes
   - Validates topics before creation

2. **AIDA Article Template** (0:10:20)
   - Headline formulas (3 types)
   - Introduction formulas (RPT, TRP, CPR)
   - Body copy structure
   - Requires 6th grade reading level only

3. **Three Jabs Strategy** (0:18:48)
   - Three content upgrades per blog post
   - Converts >10% of traffic to leads
   - 65% of opt-ins come from these upgrades
   - Result: 10,000 leads/month after 6 months

4. **Red Packet Partnerships** (0:27:29)
   - Partnership outreach via messaging
   - 100-1,000 leads in one day
   - $0 cost per lead
   - Higher quality than SEO/paid traffic

5. **$5 Hit Records** (0:42:31)
   - $5/day ad budget
   - Set-and-forget campaigns
   - Weekly check-ins only
   - Saves $1,500+/month vs agency

6. **Viral Content Upgrades** (0:45:59)
   - Free viral sharing tool
   - Daily shares from strangers
   - No cold outreach required

7. **900 Word Emails** (0:47:52)
   - Sell $3k-$15k services via email only
   - No phone calls required
   - Present Pain vs Future Gain framework
   - Three Brains model (Child/Adult/Parent)

**Target User**: Service businesses, agencies, consultants selling retainers or high-ticket offers.

**Typical Outcomes**: Inbound leads from tier-1 brands (Amazon, HubSpot, Shopify), $3k-$15k/month clients, email list growth to 10k leads/month.

#### **Framework 4: Invisible Sales Letter (ISL) System**
A $19 AI-powered system for creating 1-page sales letters that generate 10-45 customers daily.[^4]

**Core Concept**: One page that educates AND sells simultaneously, eliminating complex funnels.

**3-Step Process**:

**Step 1: Create 1-Page ISL**
- Step-by-step breakdown of how you help customers
- Surrounded by social proof of best results
- Formula: Clarity = Certainty → No objections = Fast sales
- Shifts prospect from TOFU mindset to BOFU mindset in single page

**Step 2: Setup TOFU-BOFU Ad Campaign**
- **TOFU (Top-of-Funnel)**: Cold traffic → ISL directly (laser targeted + broad audiences)
- **BOFU (Bottom-of-Funnel)**: Retarget all engagements → Mini ISL version
- Goal: Fast sales within 7 days

**Step 3: "Operation Nowhere To Hide"**
- Place ISL link across all assets:
  - Website (exit intent popup)
  - YouTube (first line of description)
  - Email newsletters (P.S.)
  - Podcast notes (first line)
- Result: Organic sales from existing content

**Success Metrics**:
- Low-ticket ($5-$50): 500-1,000 customers/month
- High-ticket ($2,500-$60,000): 30-150 sales meetings/month
- Peak: 45 customers in single day
- Typical start: 1-2 customers/week

**Target User**: Businesses <$1M revenue tired of content grind, anyone selling $5-$60k offers.

**Typical Outcomes**: Consistent daily sales, reduced time creating content, predictable revenue, higher lead quality.

#### **Framework 5: Content Marketing Masters**
A $2,997 course teaching content scaling with optimized templates.[^1]

**Structure**: 3-stage course, 19 training videos (~4.5 hours + 14 min bonus)

**3 Stages**:
1. **Content Strategy + Topic Research** - Finding revenue-generating topics
2. **Writing + SEO + AI** - Execution with modern tools
3. **Outsourcing** - Scaling via freelance copywriters

**Target User**: Businesses ready to scale content production systematically with templates.

**Typical Outcomes**: Scalable content production, improved SEO performance, effective use of AI and outsourcing.

#### **Framework 6: Greatest Hits Content**
A $2,997 advanced course on partnership + paid traffic strategies for high-ticket businesses.[^11][^1]

**Structure**: 7-part course, 7 hours video training

**7 Components**:
1. Greatest Hits Content Survey - audience research

3. AIDA Article Template - conversion-optimized writing
4. Three Jabs Strategy - lead capture mechanisms
5. Red Packet Partnerships - distribution via partners
6. Paid Traffic Strategies - profitable ads to content
7. Email Follow-up Systems - converting leads to clients

**Target User**: Business owners selling high-ticket products/services ($2k-$60k).

**Typical Outcomes**: Cheap, low-effort, long-term lead gen system, partnership-driven growth, 6-7 figures in sales from single pieces of content.[^11]

#### **Framework 7: Content Mavericks Elite**
Done-for-you + 1-on-1 coaching program with "Invisible Sales Letter" technique.[^1]

**Structure**: Custom pricing, comprehensive service

**Deliverables**:
- Invisible Sales Letter (written for you)

- Guarantee: Team works until first client acquired

**Target User**: High-commitment clients wanting full implementation support.

**Typical Outcomes**: Hands-off lead generation, professional ISL copy, managed ad campaigns.

### 2. Cross-Framework Module Breakdown

#### **Module: Greatest Hits Content Survey**

**Purpose**: Validate content topics before creation by surveying audience/market.

**Inputs**:
- Email list OR access to relevant community/group
- Survey tool (3 questions max for 80%+ completion rate)
- One primary question: "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?"

**Process**:
1. Craft 60-second survey (3 questions maximum)
2. Send via email with subject line: "I need your help"
3. Follow up with "Weekend Recap" if low response
4. Promise to share findings as incentive
5. Analyze responses for common pain points

**Outputs**:
- 5-10 validated blog post topics
- Audience pain point insights
- Topic prioritization data
- Content angle ideas

**Dependencies**: None (can be first step)

**Prompt Templates**:
```
Survey Design Prompt:
"Create a 3-question Greatest Hits Content Survey for [ICP description] 
in the [industry] space. We sell [offer description] and want to identify 
the most pressing content topics. Follow the 60-second completion principle 
and include incentive to share findings. Output: Email copy + survey questions."

Survey Analysis Prompt:
"Analyze these Greatest Hits Content Survey responses [paste responses]. 
Identify: (1) Top 5 most common pain points, (2) 5 specific blog post topics 
ranked by revenue potential, (3) Unique angles not covered by competitors, 
(4) Quote snippets for social proof. ICP: [description]. Offer: [description]."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Response rate, completion rate, topic diversity score, pain point frequency.

#### **Module: Topic Triangle Framework**

**Purpose**: Find revenue-generating topics with traffic potential before writing.

**Inputs**:
- SEO tool access (Ahrefs, SEMrush, etc.)
- Competitor URLs
- Business offer details
- Revenue potential criteria (e.g., CPC>$10)

**Process**:
1. Input competitor URLs into SEO tool
2. Filter keywords by CPC>$10 (or CPC>$5 if struggling)
3. Sort by traffic potential (highest first)
4. Create spreadsheet with Revenue Potential (1-3) x Traffic Potential
5. Find 10+ "Level 3" revenue potential topics
6. Match search intent (Google SERP analysis)

**Outputs**:
- Prioritized topic spreadsheet
- 10+ Level 3 topics
- Search intent classification per topic
- Competition analysis per topic

**Dependencies**: 
- Best if done after Greatest Hits Survey
- Required before AIDA Article Template

**Prompt Templates**:
```
Topic Triangle Analysis Prompt:
"I have these [N] keyword opportunities from competitor analysis [paste data]. 
Apply Topic Triangle framework: Calculate Revenue Potential (1-3) based on 
CPC [list CPCs], calculate Traffic Potential based on search volume [list volumes]. 
Prioritize top 10 topics where Revenue Potential x Traffic Potential is maximized. 
Business: [description]. Offer: [price/type]. Output: Ranked spreadsheet with rationale."

Search Intent Matching Prompt:
"For topic '[specific topic]' with keyword '[keyword]', analyze Google SERP 
to determine: (1) Primary search intent (informational/commercial/transactional), 
(2) SERP features present, (3) Content format that ranks #1-3 (list/guide/comparison), 
(4) Opportunity gaps in existing content, (5) Recommended content angle to outrank. 
Output: Intent profile + content strategy."

Opportunity Gap Finder Prompt:
"Analyze top 10 ranking URLs for keyword '[keyword]': [paste URLs]. 
Identify 4 opportunity gaps using criteria: (1) Outdated information, 
(2) Missing subtopics, (3) Lack of examples/case studies, (4) Poor UX/design. 
Recommend how our content can fill these gaps. Our advantage: [unique asset]. 
Output: Gap analysis + content blueprint."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Topics identified, Level 3 topics count, revenue potential score, traffic potential score, topics published.

#### **Module: AIDA Article Template**

**Purpose**: Write high-converting blog posts that attract ideal clients and convert to leads.

**Inputs**:
- Validated topic from Topic Triangle
- Target client profile (ICP)
- Call-to-action offer
- 6th grade reading level writing ability

**Process**:
1. **Attention (Headline)**: Use one of 3 headline formulas
2. **Interest (Introduction)**: Use RPT, TRP, or CPR formula to hook readers for 5+ minutes
3. **Desire (Body Copy)**: Follow structure that demonstrates expertise
4. **Action (CTA)**: Place content upgrade strategically

**Headline Formulas**:
- Number + Adjective + Target Keyword + Promise
- How to [Desired Outcome] Without [Common Objection]
- [Time Period] Way to [Desired Outcome]

**Introduction Formulas**:
- **RPT**: Result → Problem → Transformation
- **TRP**: Transformation → Result → Problem
- **CPR**: Curiosity → Problem → Result

**Outputs**:
- Complete blog post draft (structured for conversion)
- Headline options (3 variants)
- Introduction hook
- Strategic CTA placement
- Content upgrade recommendation

**Dependencies**:
- Requires Topic Triangle analysis
- Best paired with Three Jabs Strategy for CTAs

**Prompt Templates**:
```
AIDA Article Headline Generator:
"Generate 10 headline options for blog post on '[topic]' targeting [ICP description]. 
Use these 3 formulas: (1) Number + Adjective + Target Keyword + Promise, 
(2) How to [Desired Outcome] Without [Common Objection], (3) [Time Period] Way to 
[Desired Outcome]. Optimize for CTR and client attraction. Desired outcome: [outcome]. 
Common objections: [list]. Output: 10 headlines with formula labels."

AIDA Article Introduction Writer:
"Write 3 introduction variations for blog post '[headline]' using: 
(1) RPT formula (Result → Problem → Transformation), (2) TRP formula 
(Transformation → Result → Problem), (3) CPR formula (Curiosity → Problem → Result). 
Target: [ICP]. Goal: Hook readers for 5+ minutes, prevent bounce. Tone: 6th grade 
reading level, conversational, credible. Output: 3 intro variations ~150 words each."

AIDA Article Body Structurer:
"Create detailed outline for blog post '[title]' targeting [ICP] searching for [intent]. 
Follow AIDA structure: Interest sections (3-5) demonstrating expertise through 
[examples/case studies/data], Desire section showing transformation, Action section 
with strategic CTA to [offer]. Include: H2/H3 hierarchy, talking points per section, 
internal link opportunities, content upgrade placement. Output: Complete outline."

AIDA Article Full Draft:
"Write complete blog post following this outline [paste outline]. Apply AIDA 
framework: Attention (headline already set), Interest (hook + 3-5 educational sections), 
Desire (transformation/social proof), Action (CTA to [offer]). Requirements: 6th grade 
reading level, 2,000-3,000 words, conversational tone, actionable advice, strategic 
content upgrade CTAs. ICP: [description]. Output: Full draft in markdown."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Time on page, bounce rate, scroll depth, conversion rate to content upgrade, client inquiries per post.

#### **Module: Three Jabs Strategy**

**Purpose**: Convert >10% of blog post traffic into email leads through strategic content upgrades.

**Inputs**:
- Published blog post (from AIDA template)
- Understanding of reader's next logical step
- Lead capture tool (email service)
- Content upgrade creation ability

**Process**:
1. Identify 3 "jabs" (value delivery points) in blog post
2. Create content upgrades that are:
   - Next logical step from post content
   - Friction removers for implementation
   - Specific to post topic (not generic lead magnet)
3. Place CTAs strategically:
   - Beginning of post (small CTA)
   - Mid-post (inline upgrade)
   - End of post (final CTA)
4. Use lead capture tool for opt-ins

**The 3 Jabs**:
1. **Jab 1**: Small ask at beginning (e.g., "Get the checklist")
2. **Jab 2**: Value-add in middle (e.g., "Download the template")
3. **Jab 3**: Implementation tool at end (e.g., "Get the complete toolkit")

**Outputs**:
- 3 content upgrade assets per blog post
- Lead capture form copy
- Email welcome sequence trigger
- 10% minimum conversion rate (goal: 10-25%)
- 65% of total opt-ins from content upgrades

**Dependencies**:
- Requires completed AIDA Article
- Feeds into 900 Word Email module

**Prompt Templates**:
```
Three Jabs Strategy Designer:
"For blog post '[title]' about [topic], design a Three Jabs Strategy with 3 content 
upgrades. Post teaches [main points]. ICP: [description]. Requirements: Each upgrade 
must be (1) next logical step, (2) friction remover for implementation, (3) topic-specific. 
Output: 3 upgrade concepts with titles, descriptions, placement locations, and 
conversion hypothesis."

Content Upgrade Creator - Checklist:
"Create a content upgrade checklist for blog post '[title]'. Post covers [main points]. 
Checklist should: (1) Distill post into actionable steps, (2) Include 7-12 items, 
(3) Be printable/saveable, (4) Reference specific post sections. Format: PDF-ready 
markdown with checkboxes. Output: Complete checklist with compelling title."

Content Upgrade Creator - Template:
"Create a content upgrade template for blog post '[title]'. Post teaches [process]. 
Template should: (1) Provide fill-in-the-blank framework, (2) Include examples, 
(3) Reference post methodology, (4) Be immediately actionable. ICP: [description]. 
Format: Editable document. Output: Complete template with instructions."

Content Upgrade Creator - Toolkit:
"Create a comprehensive content upgrade toolkit for blog post '[title]'. Post covers 
[topics]. Toolkit should include: (1) Quick-start guide, (2) Resource list, 
(3) Implementation checklist, (4) Bonus tips not in post, (5) Next steps. 
Goal: Move reader from learning to implementation. Output: Complete toolkit outline + copy."

Lead Capture Copy Writer:
"Write lead capture form copy for content upgrade '[upgrade name]' related to blog 
post '[title]'. Include: (1) Headline (benefit-driven), (2) 2-3 bullet points 
(outcome-focused), (3) Button text (action-oriented), (4) Privacy assurance, 
(5) A/B test variant. Tone: Conversational, low-friction. Output: 2 complete form copy options."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Overall conversion rate (traffic to leads), conversion rate per upgrade, opt-in source breakdown (which of 3 jabs performs best), leads per post per month.

#### **Module: Red Packet Partnerships**

**Purpose**: Generate 100-1,000 high-quality leads in one day through strategic content partnerships.

**Inputs**:
- Completed high-value blog post
- Target partner criteria (audience size, niche alignment)
- Partnership value proposition
- Outreach messaging template

**Process**:
1. **Identify Partners**: Find people with 10k+ audience in your niche
   - Method 1: LinkedIn search by title + industry
   - Method 2: Podcast guest rosters
   - Method 3: Conference speaker lists
   - Method 4: Facebook group admins
   - Method 5: Newsletter directories
   - Method 6: YouTube channel search
2. **Craft Outreach**: Personalized message offering value
3. **Provide Assets**: Give partner everything needed to promote
4. **Track Results**: Monitor lead source from each partner

**The "Red Packet" Concept**: Give partners a valuable "gift" (your content) that benefits their audience, creating reciprocity.

**Outputs**:
- Partner target list (50-100 prospects)
- Personalized outreach messages
- Partner promotion assets (social copy, email copy, graphics)
- 100-1,000 leads in single day
- $0 cost per lead
- Higher quality leads than SEO/paid traffic

**Dependencies**:
- Requires proven blog post (ideally with Three Jabs Strategy)
- Works best after content has social proof (shares, comments)

**Prompt Templates**:
```
Partnership Target Finder:
"Identify 50 potential Red Packet Partners for promoting content about [topic]. 
Our ICP: [description]. Partner criteria: (1) 10k+ audience, (2) [niche] focus, 
(3) Active engagement, (4) Not direct competitor. Provide: Name, platform, 
audience size estimate, why good fit. Use these sources: [LinkedIn/podcasts/conferences/etc.]. 
Output: Prioritized partner list with contact approach."

Partnership Outreach Message:
"Write Red Packet Partnership outreach message to [partner name] who [brief background]. 
Our content: '[blog post title]' about [topic]. Value for their audience: [specific benefit]. 
Message must: (1) Personalize opening (reference their work), (2) Offer value first, 
(3) Make ask small and specific, (4) Provide social proof, (5) Make promotion easy. 
Tone: Collaborative, not salesy. Platform: [Facebook Messenger/Email/LinkedIn]. 
Output: Complete message ~150 words."

Partnership Promotion Assets:
"Create Red Packet Partnership promotion assets for content '[title]'. Partner: [name].

platforms), (2) Email to their list (subject + body), (3) 3 key talking points, 
(4) Why their audience will benefit (3 bullets), (5) Tracking link instructions.

Partnership Follow-up Sequence:
"Create Red Packet Partnership follow-up sequence after initial outreach to [partner name]. 
Scenario: [No response / Interested but slow / Needs more info / etc.]. Sequence: 
3 follow-up messages over 2 weeks. Each must: (1) Add new value/insight, (2) Reduce 
friction further, (3) Respect their time, (4) Provide easy exit. Tone: Helpful, not pushy. 
Output: 3 messages with timing."

Partnership Results Analyzer:
"Analyze Red Packet Partnership campaign results. Partners contacted: [N]. Responses: [N]. 
Promotions completed: [N]. Leads generated: [N]. Cost: $[amount]. Lead quality indicators: 
[data]. Create: (1) Partner performance leaderboard, (2) ROI analysis, (3) Insights on 
what messaging worked, (4) Recommendations for next campaign. Output: Complete analysis + action items."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Partners contacted, response rate, promotion agreement rate, leads per partner, cost per lead, lead-to-customer conversion rate (to prove quality).

#### **Module: 900 Word Emails**

**Purpose**: Convert leads into $3k-$15k/month clients through strategic email follow-up without phone calls.

**Inputs**:
- Email list from Three Jabs Strategy
- Client offer details ($500-$15k price point)
- Understanding of prospect's present pain
- Client success stories (social proof)

**Process**:
1. **Timing**: Send 2-3 days after content upgrade opt-in
2. **Structure**: Follow 900-word format
   - Opening: Acknowledge their situation (empathy)
   - Present Pain: Focus on current suffering (not future gain)
   - Solution: Show how you solve the pain
   - Social Proof: Share client result story
   - Offer: Make irresistible, low-friction offer
   - CTA: Single, clear next step
3. **Tone**: Talk to three brains (Child/Adult/Parent)
4. **Follow-up**: If interested, continue via email or book call

**Three Brains Model**:
- **Child Brain**: Emotional, wants instant gratification
- **Adult Brain**: Logical, weighs pros/cons
- **Parent Brain**: Protective, worried about risks

**Present Pain vs Future Gain**:
- ❌ Future Gain: "Use my magic pill to lose 10 pounds in 10 weeks"
- ✅ Present Pain: "Tired of feeling uncomfortable in your clothes right now?"

**Outputs**:
- High-converting sales email (~900 words)
- 50% of recipients won't buy (realistic expectation)
- $3k-$15k clients from email alone
- Response rate: 5-20% for qualified offers
- Sales without phone call requirement

**Dependencies**:
- Requires leads from Three Jabs Strategy
- Best results when blog post has built trust first

**Prompt Templates**:
```
900 Word Email - Present Pain Identifier:
"Identify the present pain for [ICP] related to [problem]. They've just read our blog 
post '[title]' and downloaded [content upgrade]. What pain are they feeling RIGHT NOW 
(not future consequences)? Consider: (1) Daily frustrations, (2) Emotional toll, 
(3) Immediate obstacles, (4) Current sacrifices. Output: 5 present pain statements 
ranked by intensity."

900 Word Email - Full Draft:
"Write a 900-word sales email to convert [ICP] who downloaded '[content upgrade]' into 
client for [offer at $X price]. Follow structure: (1) Empathy opening (acknowledge situation), 
(2) Present pain focus (current suffering), (3) Solution overview (how we solve it), 
(4) Social proof story (client result), (5) Irresistible offer (low friction), 
(6) Single CTA. Address all three brains (Child/Adult/Parent). Present pain: [pain]. 
Offer details: [scope, price, guarantee]. Client story: [brief result]. Tone: Conversational, 
authoritative, empathetic. Output: Complete email."

900 Word Email - Three Brains Optimizer:
"Optimize this sales email [paste email] to speak to all three brains. Current version 
addresses: [Child/Adult/Parent - identify which]. Add: (1) Child brain elements 
(emotional triggers, instant gratification), (2) Adult brain elements (logic, ROI, process), 
(3) Parent brain elements (risk reversal, safety, protection). Maintain ~900 words. 
Output: Optimized email with brain-targeted elements labeled."

900 Word Email - Objection Destroyer:
"This sales email [paste email] sells [offer]. Identify top 5 objections [ICP] will have 
and weave objection handling into email. Objections to address: (1) Price, (2) Time, 
(3) Effort required, (4) Risk/guarantee, (5) Fit/suitability. Handle objections naturally 
(not FAQ style). Output: Email version with embedded objection handling."

900 Word Email - Follow-up Sequence:
"Create 3-email follow-up sequence after initial 900-word sales email (no response). 
Original email sold [offer at $X]. Each follow-up must: (1) Add new value/angle, 
(2) Re-state offer differently, (3) Include different social proof, (4) Respect non-interest 
signals. Timing: Email 2 at day 3, Email 3 at day 7, Email 4 at day 14. Tone: Persistent 
but respectful. Output: 3 complete follow-up emails."
```

**Metrics to Track**: Email open rate, response rate, sales calls booked, clients closed via email only, revenue per email sent, time to close.

#### **Module: Invisible Sales Letter (ISL) Creation**

**Purpose**: Create a single 1-page sales letter that educates prospects and sells simultaneously.

**Inputs**:
- Step-by-step process of how you help customers
- 5-10 client success stories with specific results
- Offer details (price, scope, deliverables, guarantee)
- Ideal customer profile and their pain points

**Process**:
1. **Opening**: Problem agitation (what reader is experiencing)
2. **Education**: Step-by-step breakdown of your process
   - Each step explained clearly
