### 3. Los 3 Pilares Estratégicos

Mapear cada persona a un pilar. Si el equipo es ≤3, una persona cubre múltiples pilares con apoyo de AI.

| Pilar | Responsabilidad | Owner | AI Support | KPI |
|-------|-----------------|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Demand** | Revenue + Pipeline | | copywriting-engine, funnel-optimization | MQLs, Pipeline $, Win Rate |
| **Brand** | Awareness + Engagement | | content-strategy, alma | Traffic, Engagement, Share of Voice |
| **Product Marketing** | Positioning + Enablement | | positioning, icp-analysis | Win Rate, Sales Cycle Length |

### 5. Sistema Anti-Silos

| Ritmo | Frecuencia | Participantes | Objetivo |
|-------|------------|---------------|----------|
| Daily standup | Diario, 10 min | Todo el equipo | "Qué hago hoy, qué necesito" |
| Weekly review | Semanal, 30 min | Todo el equipo + ventas | Métricas + siguiente semana |
| Monthly strategy | Mensual, 60 min | Equipo + liderazgo | Revenue vs target + ajustes |

### 7. Plan de Acción 12 Semanas

| Semana | Foco | Entregable | Owner |
|--------|------|-----------|-------|
| 1-2 | Discovery | Strategy alignment + resource audit | PM + equipo |
| 3-6 | Demand Modeling | Revenue target breakdown + funnel model | Demand owner |
| 7-10 | Team Design | Role definition por pilar + agency decisions | PM |
| 11-12 | Integration | Communication rhythms + shared dashboards | Todo el equipo |

### 8. Growth Loops del Equipo

- **Loop 1 — Revenue-Driven Resource Allocation**: Monthly reviews → budget/people reallocation to high-performing channels
- **Loop 2 — Cross-Pillar Learning**: Success in one pillar documented and adapted by others
- **Loop 3 — CEO Partnership Momentum**: Regular vision alignment → faster approvals + executive advocacy
